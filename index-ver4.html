Routing Rules & Quick Reference

Use the sections below for quick reference. Open only the rule set you need.

QS = Qualifying Studies FSO = Future Students AAC = Academic Advising Centre
Peer Advisors
Availability: Fall and Winter semesters. Off for Summer semester.

Peer Advisors can only meet with:
  • Only BSc students for Science slots and BA students for Arts slots.
  • Only students with 60 credits or less.
  • Only students with a GPA of 2.0 or above.
  • Students must be alone.
Main duties: Basic inquiries such as course planning, declaring a major/minor, using myUFV and myGradplan.
General Routing Rules
  • If switching from one program to another, students meet with an advisor for the program they are switching to, with the QS exceptions noted below.
  • Students meet with the program advisor for their major.
  • If questions are about a minor in a program AAC does not advise for, such as Business or Criminology, refer the student to that department.

AI&ML PB: Students in the AI&ML post-baccalaureate program meet with Science advisors. This applies at any credit level — the Arts/Science 0–30 → QS rule does not apply to AI&ML PB students.

Modern Languages: Students in Modern Languages see Arts unless they are in another program and taking a MOLA certificate concurrently. In that case, their current program advisor advises them.

QS / switching exceptions:
  • Students with 0–30 credits in BA, AA, LA, BSc or ASc → QS. Exception: AI&ML PB students meet with Science advisors even with fewer than 30 credits.
  • Students switching to Nursing/Health Sciences who are not enrolled → QS.
  • Students switching to Nursing Track → QS.
  • Students switching to Trades → QS.
  • Students wanting to switch programs but unsure to what program → QS.
  • Students in ABE switching to an academic program → QS.
Returning students:
  • 0–60 credits in a degree → QS.
  • 0–30 credits in a diploma → QS.
  • Any credits when switching programs → QS.
  • 60+ credits for a degree, or 30+ credits for a diploma, and not switching programs → program advisor.
  • Returning students wanting to switch to Trades or Health Sciences → QS.
Programs / Services AAC Does Not Advise
  • Upgrading (ABE): unless for 100-level classes, then book with QS.
  • Business and Criminology: refer to the appropriate department.
  • Trades: if enrolled in a Trades program and seeking advice on that program, refer to Trades School Coordinators.
  • Health Sciences: enrolled students → Health Sciences.
  • School of Education: enrolled students → School of Education. See the list of requirements for when to meet with an SED advisor.
  • Continuing Education: contact Continuing Education.
  • Future students: refer to Future Students / FSO.
Other Requests
  • PLAR requests: refer to UFV PLAR. Advisors can assist with informational questions.
  • TQS teacher credits: requests go to the program their degree is with (e.g., Arts → Arts).
  • Associate Communications Certificate declaration requests: Arts advisors.
  • Prerequisite waivers: go to individual departments, such as KIN. Refer the student to the department coordinator.
  • Post-baccalaureate diplomas: if graduate level / 400+, send to the department. If unsure, ask advisors.
Prototype only. Routing rules should be validated by AAC/advising leadership before being used for real student routing. Advisor availability and appointment slots are not automatically checked.
