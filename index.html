Routing Rules

The routing engine uses the student's status, current program, intended program and completed credits. Appointment availability is not assumed.

Arts / Science 0–30 Students in the applicable Arts and Science/Computer programs with 0–30 completed credits → QS.
Program Switching Students switching programs normally go to the advisor for the destination program, subject to QS exceptions.
Returning Students Returning students with lower credit totals may be routed to QS. Students with higher credits who are not switching normally return to their program advisor.
QS Exceptions Switching into Nursing, Nursing Track, Trades or an unknown destination → QS.
Special Programs Business, Criminology, Health Sciences, Trades, School of Education and Continuing Education follow their respective departments/services.
Program-Based Routing Arts, Science/Computer Information Systems, BKIN, Fine Arts and Social Work/CYC are routed according to their program group.
Prototype only. Routing rules is for the Front Desk team & should be approved first by AAC management.
